# Quick Eats Food Delivery Performance Analysis (Bengaluru)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on identifying the main causes of late food deliveries for **Quick Eats** in Bengaluru, India, during the period of January - March 2026. The platform received severe customer complaints regarding operational delays. This diagnostic analysis isolates critical bottlenecks to improve delivery performance and customer retention.

---

## 📊 Dataset Profile
- **Total Orders Analyzed:** 50,000+
- **Fulfillment Failure Rate:** 32% late delivery rate across all orders.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings

### 1. Delivery Delay by Time of Day
Peak bottleneck hours occur heavily during the dinner rush hour.
- **6:00 PM - 9:00 PM (Dinner Period):** **43% of orders are delayed** (Highest overall).
- **9:00 PM - 12:00 AM:** 35% delay rate.

### 2. Location & Vendor Bottlenecks
- **Electronic City** recorded the highest geographic delay rate with **44% of deliveries marked late**.
- **Spice Hub** was identified as the worst-performing restaurant partner, averaging an overall late-delivery metric.
- Orders covering a distance of **12+ km** average a steep **62-minute delivery time**.

### 3. Impact on Customer Satisfaction
Logistical delays are directly tied to sharp drops in user ratings:
- **Under 30 mins:** 4.4 / 5 average rating.
- **Above 50 mins:** Drops heavily to a **2.9 / 5 rating**.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Dynamic Fleet Management:** Increase active delivery partners by 15-20% specifically during the 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM window.
2. **Geographic Re-balancing:** Re-route courier clusters closer to Electronic City to handle high peak-volumes.
3. **Vendor Logistics Caps:** Limit maximum delivery radiuses for high-delay partners like *Spice Hub* to prevent long-distance courier locking.
